About usስለ እኛ

The Light of God Church is an Ethiopian and Eritrean Evangelical Church in Karlsruhe, Germany.

Who we are

የእግዚአብሔር ብርሃን ቤተ ክርስቲያን (The Light of God Church) is an Ethiopian and Eritrean Evangelical Church located in Karlsruhe, Germany.

We worship together every Sunday in Amharic, Tigrinya and German, and we welcome all believers seeking God’s presence, fellowship and spiritual growth.

Our mission is to share the light of God through worship, prayer and service.

Our languages

  • አማርኛAmharic
  • ትግርኛTigrinya
  • DeutschGerman

Our mission

Spread the gospel

To spread the gospel of Jesus Christ.

Support families

To support Ethiopian and Eritrean Christian families.

Build unity

To build unity, love and spiritual growth in our community.

What we believe

As an evangelical church, we hold to the core of the Christian faith.

  • The Bible is God’s Word and our authority for faith and life.
  • There is one God, revealed as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.
  • Jesus Christ, God’s Son, died for our sins and rose from the dead.
  • We are saved by grace through faith in Jesus Christ.
  • The Holy Spirit lives in believers and helps us follow Jesus.
  • Jesus will return, and we wait for him with hope.
