Welcome

እንኳን ወደ የእግዚአብሔር ብርሃን ቤተ ክርስቲያን በካርልስሩኸ በደህና መጡ።

Welcome to The Light of God Church, an Ethiopian and Eritrean Evangelical Church in Karlsruhe. We serve the Ethiopian and Eritrean Christian community in Germany.

Join us for Sunday services, Bible study and fellowship. We welcome all believers seeking God’s presence, fellowship and spiritual growth.

About our church

  • ትግርኛTigrinya
